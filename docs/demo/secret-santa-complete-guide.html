What this guide covers

Share this with an organizer before the exchange, or send it to members after invites go out.

My Secret Santa separates the exchange into simple moments. The organizer creates and manages the group. Each participant adds wishlist clues, checks their giftee after names are drawn, and updates gift progress when something changes.

1
Prepare the exchange
Decide the group name, gift day, budget, privacy choices, and invite list.
2
Invite members
Send invites and wait for people to join before drawing names.
3
Collect wishlist clues
Everyone adds useful gift hints, links, notes, and budget-friendly ideas.
4
Draw names privately
The organizer draws names. Each member only sees their own giftee.
5
Plan and track gifts
Use wishlist clues, shopping ideas, private messages, and gift progress updates.
6
Run reveal day
Reveal safely when the group is ready, then keep the exchange in history.
Roles

Who does what

Secret Santa works best when the organizer and members know their own jobs.
Organizer

What you are responsible for

  • Create the group and set the date, budget, and privacy choices.
  • Invite members and check who has joined.
  • Encourage members to add wishlist clues before the draw.
  • Draw names only when the group is ready.
  • Use reminders and reveal controls without exposing matches early.
Participant

What each member does

  • Accept the invite and confirm the profile name or avatar.
  • Add enough wishlist clues so their Santa has useful options.
  • After the draw, check the giftee and gift budget.
  • Ask private questions when more clues are needed.
  • Update gift progress so the organizer knows the exchange is on track.
Best rule: do not rush the draw. Wait until the right people have joined and everyone has had a chance to add wishlist clues.
Quick path

The full exchange in ten steps

Use this as the simplest walkthrough for first-time users.
1

Sign in

Use Google or email sign-in. Your account keeps your groups, wishlists, messages, and gift progress together.

2

Create a group

Open Create Group. Add the exchange name, gift day, description, budget, privacy choices, and invites.

3

Send invites

Invite people by email or invite link. Keep an eye on who joined and who still needs a reminder.

4

Add wishlist clues

Each person opens My Wishlist and adds a few ideas, notes, links, and priority choices.

5

Review readiness

The organizer checks that members have joined, wishlists are ready enough, and the draw rules make sense.

6

Draw names

The organizer starts the draw. Each member privately receives one giftee. Other matches stay hidden.

7

Open My Giftee or Shopping Ideas

Participants review their giftee, wishlist clues, budget, and shopping options.

8

Ask quietly

If a clue is missing, use private Secret Santa messages so the question stays anonymous before reveal day.

9

Update gift progress

Mark progress as you choose, buy, wrap, or deliver a gift. Confirm received gifts only after they arrive.

10

Reveal and remember

On gift day, use the reveal flow when the group is ready. Afterward, keep past exchanges in history.

Organizer walkthrough

Create and manage a group

This is the main flow for the person running the exchange.
1

Open Create Group

Start a new exchange from the dashboard or groups screen. Use a name people will recognize, such as Office Secret Santa 2026.

2

Fill out Basics

Add the event date, a short description, and any notes members need before joining. Keep this friendly and specific.

3

Choose the budget

Pick a preset amount or enter a custom amount. The budget appears later when each Santa chooses a gift.

4

Set privacy and invite choices

Review who can join, how invites are handled, and whether the group setup is ready for the people you plan to invite.

5

Add invitees

Use names and email addresses carefully. Send invites only to people who should be part of the exchange.

6

Review before creating

Check the name, date, budget, and invite list. It is easier to fix mistakes before the group is created.

Organizer caution: drawing names is a real group action. If you reset later, the current recipients and private chat history for that group can be removed.

Before you draw names

Every expected member has joined.
Members have at least a few wishlist clues.
The budget and gift day are correct.
No duplicate or accidental invite is still pending.

After you draw names

Tell members to open My Giftee or Shopping Ideas.
Use reminders if wishlists or gifts need attention.
Do not reveal matches early unless the group is ready.
Save reveal day for the right time.
Participant walkthrough

Join, add clues, and plan your gift

This is the flow most group members will follow.

1. Accept the invite

Open the invite from the organizer. Sign in or create an account so the group can connect your invite to you.

2. Check your profile

Use a name and festive avatar that group members can recognize. You can upload a profile photo if the app allows it.

3. Add wishlist clues

Open My Wishlist. Add several ideas with short notes. A mix of exact items and broad interests works best.

4. Wait for the draw

The organizer draws names when the group is ready. Until then, you may not have a giftee yet.

5. Open your giftee

After the draw, open My Giftee or Shopping Ideas to see the person you are gifting and their clues.

6. Choose and track the gift

Use the group budget as your guide. Update Gift progress when you choose, buy, wrap, or deliver the gift.

Wishlist tip: write clues that help a real person choose. Good examples: "coffee tools under PHP 1,000", "desk items for a small workspace", or "cozy socks, dark colors".

Good wishlist clues

  • Specific enough to guide someone.
  • Flexible enough for the budget.
  • Helpful notes about size, color, or style.

Use private messages for

  • Size questions.
  • Color or style preferences.
  • Clarifying a vague wishlist clue.

Avoid

  • Asking in a way that reveals you.
  • Adding private personal data.
  • Confirming a gift before it arrives.

Screen-reading tips

How to teach the flow without confusing people

This page is useful for demos, support, and onboarding.

For organizers

  • Start from My Groups, not from every menu item.
  • Show Create Group as a five-step setup: basics, budget, privacy, invites, review.
  • Explain that drawing names should happen only after the member list is correct.
  • Use the group workspace as the home base for invite and reveal questions.

For participants

  • Start from Dashboard to see what needs attention.
  • Add wishlist clues before the draw if possible.
  • After the draw, use My Giftee, Shopping Ideas, and Secret Messages together.
  • Update Gift Progress only for real progress, and confirm received only after arrival.

How to finish the exchange well

Reveal day

Reveal-day checklists

Use these right before the exchange becomes public to the group.

Reveal-day checklist for organizers

Confirm the group has completed the draw.
Check that gifts are ready or delivered as expected.
Open the reveal screen before everyone gathers.
Use the live reveal controls at the group's pace.
Publish the group reveal only when it is okay for everyone to see.

Reveal-day checklist for members

Bring or deliver the gift before the agreed time.
Do not announce your giftee until the reveal starts.
Confirm received gifts only after they arrive.
Join the reveal screen if the organizer shares it.
Enjoy the reveal and thank your Santa.
Plain-language reveal rule: before reveal day, keep your giftee private. After reveal day, the group can celebrate and remember who picked each name.

If something changes at the last minute

Use group messages or reminders to coordinate without spoiling matches. If a person drops out before the draw, update the group before drawing names. If the draw has already happened, the organizer should review the reset impact before changing assignments.

Privacy and etiquette

Keep the surprise safe

These rules help the app stay useful and comfortable for normal groups.

What stays private before reveal

  • Who is gifting whom.
  • Private Secret Santa questions.
  • Gift progress that should only help planning.
  • Personal account and profile details that are not needed by the group.

What people may see

  • Group name, gift day, budget, and general setup.
  • Member display names or festive avatars.
  • Wishlist clues meant to help gift planning.
  • Reveal results after the group intentionally reveals them.

Do

  • Use fake demo data when recording tutorials.
  • Keep wishlist notes respectful.
  • Use the budget as a guide.
  • Report suspicious access or privacy issues.

Do not

  • Share screenshots that reveal another person's giftee.
  • Paste secrets, account codes, or private documents into wishlists.
  • Use someone else's invite link.
  • Record real emails or private messages in public demos.

Shopping note

  • Some shopping links may be affiliate links.
  • Stores have their own checkout and privacy rules.
  • The app helps with planning, not store order support.
For privacy, deletion, or security requests, contact mysecretsanta.notifications@gmail.com.
Troubleshooting

Common questions and fixes

Use this as a quick support reference.

I do not have a group yet.

Go to My Groups or Create Group. If you are joining someone else's exchange, ask the organizer for an invite.

My invite link does not work.

It may be expired, already used, or mistyped. Ask the organizer to resend the invite or check the email address.

I do not see my giftee.

The organizer may not have drawn names yet, or you may not be in the right group. Check the group page first.

My wishlist says it needs clues.

Add at least a few clear gift ideas. Include notes about sizes, colors, interests, or links if helpful.

I forgot my password.

Use the forgot-password page. If you signed in with Google, use Google sign-in again instead of a password reset.

I cannot upload a profile photo.

Try a smaller JPG or PNG, then refresh and try again. If it still fails, use a festive avatar for now.

Shopping links confuse me.

Use them as search helpers. Always check price, store, shipping, and fit before buying.

Someone joined by mistake.

Fix the member list before drawing names. If names were already drawn, the organizer should review reset impact first.

I received no email.

Check spam, promotions, and the email address used. You can often continue by signing in and checking the dashboard.

Can the organizer see every match?

The organizer manages the exchange, but the app is designed to avoid exposing matches before reveal actions are ready.
