# My Secret Santa Demo Production Guide

This guide is the production plan for a high-quality start-to-finish demo video and matching user instructions. Use clean demo accounts only. Do not show real emails, private wishlists, affiliate account pages, tokens, or production user data.

## Deliverables

- Main walkthrough: 3 to 4 minutes, 1920x1080, calm voiceover, full captions.
- Short launch cut: 45 to 60 seconds, 1080x1920, optimized for social.
- Written guide: public `/how-it-works` page plus support copy for onboarding emails or help docs.

## Recording Setup

- Use a staging or local demo workspace with fake names: Ana, Ben, Clara, Diego, and Mia.
- Use a demo group named `Holiday Gift Circle`.
- Use a simple budget such as `PHP 1,000`.
- Use a gift day at least two weeks in the future.
- Use fake wishlist items only, such as mug warmer, journal, desk lamp, cocoa set, or board game.
- Keep browser zoom at 100 percent and record at 1920x1080.
- Hide bookmarks, browser extensions, password managers, and notifications.
- Never record Supabase, Vercel, Gmail, Amazon, Shopee, Lazada, or admin dashboards in the customer demo.

## Visual Direction

- Warm, premium, and plain-language.
- Use the app's real screens as the hero; avoid generic stock UI.
- Use short chapter cards between major moments.
- Keep the cursor movement slow and intentional.
- Avoid shaky scrolling. Stop on each screen long enough for captions to be read.
- Use soft music at low volume under voiceover.

## Written User Instructions

1. Sign in with Google or email.
2. Create a group and add the exchange name.
3. Set the gift day, budget, privacy choices, and invites.
4. Ask members to join and add wishlist clues.
5. Draw names when everyone is ready.
6. Open My Giftee to see who you are gifting.
7. Use Shopping Ideas and private messages to plan the gift.
8. Update Gift Progress as you choose, buy, wrap, and deliver.
9. Use the reveal screen on gift day.
10. Revisit finished exchanges in History.

## Quality Checklist

- The video uses fake names and fake wishlist data.
- No real email addresses, tokens, keys, affiliate credentials, or private records appear.
- Captions match the voiceover.
- The cursor never covers important text.
- Each screen is readable on mobile and desktop playback.
- The final video has a title, description, thumbnail, and a link to `/how-it-works`.
- The support article and video script use normal user terms, not backend or database terms.
